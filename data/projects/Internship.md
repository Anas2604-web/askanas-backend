# Hosho Digital — Internship

## One-line summary

Jr. Consultant / Developer Intern at Hosho Digital, a consulting firm, June–July 2026 — delivered CRX Kit (enterprise CRM on Power Platform, later independently rebuilt pro-code) and an HR Analytics pipeline on Microsoft Fabric.

## Note

This is a summary of the Hosho Digital internship itself, not an individually-ranked project — see the CRX Kit and Fabric Pipelines docs for the ranked deliverables produced during this internship.

## Context

Structured as a fixed-term, selection-phase internship with defined deliverables rather than an open-ended role — not a live paying-client engagement, but full ownership over schema design, workflow automation, and AI integration decisions, the way a real consulting deliverable would work.

## What was built

1. **CRX Kit** — an enterprise CRM on Microsoft's Power Platform spanning Contact Management, Sales Management, and Marketing Automation, on a relational Dataverse schema, with an AI Builder/OpenAI layer for customer-summary generation, sentiment analysis, and deal win-probability prediction. Independently extended beyond the assigned scope with a full pro-code rebuild (.NET Core 8, EF Core, PostgreSQL, React, Groq-powered assistant). See the CRX Kit doc for full detail.

2. **HR Analytics Pipeline** — built on Microsoft Fabric using Bronze-Silver-Gold Medallion architecture: Dataflow Gen2 ingestion, Power Query cleansing, star-schema Gold layer feeding Power BI dashboards. See the Fabric Pipelines doc for full detail.

## Why the internship ended when it did

Fixed-term internship tied to specific deliverables, both completed within the internship timeline. Not performance-related — Hosho's full-time hiring was tied to open client-account seats at the time, and there wasn't one available when the internship concluded.

## What it demonstrated

Ability to work across both low-code enterprise tooling (Power Platform) and full data-engineering pipelines (Fabric) within the same short engagement, plus the initiative to go beyond the assigned low-code deliverable with an independent pro-code rebuild.
