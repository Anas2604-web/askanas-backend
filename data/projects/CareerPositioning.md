# Career Positioning

## One-line summary
Anas is targeting Applied AI Engineer and Full-Stack Engineer roles — specifically the intersection of the two: shipping production software that integrates LLMs, RAG, and agentic systems, rather than pure ML research or model training roles.

## What "Applied AI Engineer" means for Anas specifically
Anas builds and ships complete systems that integrate large language models into real products — RAG pipelines, agentic tool-calling, vector search, and production concerns like multi-tenancy, cost optimization, and evaluation. This is distinct from ML Engineer or Data Scientist roles, which focus on training and fine-tuning models from scratch — an area Anas has deliberately not pursued, favoring the application/systems layer where his software engineering background gives him a real edge.

## Why this combination (full-stack + AI) rather than one or the other
Most engineers are strong in one direction: either they can ship production full-stack systems, or they understand LLM/RAG architecture, rarely both well. Anas's projects demonstrate both simultaneously — DevConnect and CRX Kit prove full production full-stack delivery (auth, payments, real-time, deployed with real users), while RepoMind, DevDocs AI, and AskAnas prove deep, hands-on RAG and agentic system design, including production concerns like multi-tenant isolation and cost reduction, not just demo-level AI integration.

## Why not pure ML/Research roles
Anas has intentionally focused on the application and systems layer of AI (RAG, agents, LLM orchestration, production deployment) rather than model training, fine-tuning, or research-oriented ML work. This is a deliberate scope decision, not a skill gap — reflecting where the current job market for freshers is strongest and where his engineering background is most directly applicable.

## AskAnas itself as evidence
AskAnas — this very agent — is itself a demonstration of the skill set: a deployed, real-user-facing agentic RAG system built with LangGraph, Qdrant, and FastAPI, answering real questions in production. It's both a portfolio piece and a working example of the exact kind of system an Applied AI Engineer role would expect someone to build.

## How to talk about this in an interview
If asked "why not go into ML/data science," the honest answer: the freshest, most in-demand path into AI right now for someone from a software engineering background is the applied/product layer — integrating and orchestrating foundation models into real systems — not training models from scratch, which typically requires a research background Anas hasn't pursued and isn't the direction he wants to go. His projects reflect a consistent, deliberate bet on that applied layer, not a lack of exposure to ML fundamentals.
